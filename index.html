Our story

Built from the ground up, one home at a time

Yenny's Cleaning didn't start with investors or a franchise. It started with Marcela and her mom, cleaning houses for someone else for almost two years. Marcela paid attention to everything — what great cleaning looks like, how to treat a home, how to earn a client's trust — and learned all she could.

Then she took the leap and opened her own company. Her partner, Jazmín, joined her, and together they started small and grew the honest way: 3 houses, then 10, then 15 — every new client earned by doing the last job right.

When the chance came to clean vacation homes, they took it — and found their calling. Today Yenny's Cleaning focuses on vacation rental turnovers and commercial cleaning across Bend and Sunriver, with the same rule they started with: treat every space like it matters, because to someone, it does.
